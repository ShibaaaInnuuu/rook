ETH → ckETH

Turns ETH into ckETH on the Internet Computer for one IC principal. From Ethereum it calls DFINITY's ckETH helper directly. From another chain, Relay bridges the ETH and makes that same helper call on Ethereum, in one transaction you sign. About 20 minutes after the deposit lands on Ethereum, the principal holds the ckETH. Your wallet shows every transaction before you sign it.

At most 0.05 ETH from this page. 0.004 is about 3.3 ICP, about 7 T cycles at today's rates. From another chain you pay this plus Relay's fee, about 1 %.
