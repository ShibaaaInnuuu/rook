## Findings and what was done

| ID | Finding | Severity | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | A link's undo restores a whole-collection snapshot taken at `begin`, so any other book write made while the link is open is wiped when the link fails or never lands (advances, settlements, tickets, raffle state) | SEV1 | Every non-link writer waits while the collection has an open link: `desk::state::collection_busy`, checked by `ethflow/home_books::send`, `frogsol/books::send`, the cranks' `distribute` (all collections), `resolve`, the raffle round. The inverse-delta undo stays a possible later refinement. |
| F2 | `distribute` credited a module's surplus in the books before (or without) the chain paying; the reconciliation then halts the collection | SEV1 | `Books::distribute_owed` / `distribute_all_owed` (the cranks' form): the surplus is only owed; the transfer `K_tre → M[i]` is a chain link (`TreasuryStep::Surplus { link }`, `OpKind::Sweep`, `Effects { lamports_in }`) whose booking (`fund_reserve`) is gated by `pre_sign` and undone if the transfer fails or never lands. The books' own `distribute` (rook_core's semantics, the audit regressions) is unchanged. |
| F3 | Pool-desk sweeps paid the treasury in wSOL while every treasury payment is native SOL | SEV1 | Sweeps pay native (`transfer(K_desk → K_tre)`, bounded by the desk's native above its float and rent); the desk keeps its wSOL as working capital. |
| F4 | A landed raffle claim whose books claim then refused was paid again every pass | SEV1 (conditional) | A landed claim always clears the pending claim; a refusal becomes a logged wait keyed `claim_paid:<coll>:<round>` for the operator. |
| F5 | `expire_raffle` dropped a pending claim while its transfer was in flight | SEV2 | No expiry while a `Claim` for that collection is the crank line's pending step. |
| F6 | `distribute_all` was not all-or-nothing | SEV2 | Run on a copy of the books, kept only when every index distributes. |
| F7 | The prize link was begun before two more awaits | SEV2 | Every key is derived before `begin`. |
| F8 | A books refusal inside the pool desk's `decide` aborted the collection's pass every pass with no wait | SEV3 | The refusal is the step's wait (`WAIT_RETRY_SECS`); the later steps still run. |
| F9 | The desk's orphan check only looked at its own line, paging a false STUCK for pool/treasury/coins links | SEV3 | `op_id_of` maps the pool, coins and treasury steps' links; the crash-recovery pass checks every line. |
| F10 | A coins step that failed to sign for a non-simulation reason spun every pass | SEV3 | A short wait (5 min) for that step. |
| F11 | `Books::skim` and `Books::refund` (`OpKind::Remint`) have no caller: revenue above target reaches the treasury only through fee cashes; a refunded ticket cannot be re-minted | Informational | Two treasury steps on the crank line, both links: `Skim` (`M[i] → K_tre`, `Books::skim` booked as income at begin, hourly scan, 0.01 SOL bound) and `Remint` (ONE wX minted to the ticket's owner once the ledger's refund rules say so — a resolved ticket marked undeliverable or never sent past its grace; a pinned ticket only while its round is still ahead, A2-04). |
| F12 | `ClaimerSweep` reads the claimer's balance without a stated commitment | Informational | Checked: `sol_accounts` is `getMultipleAccounts` at `finalized` (`solrpc.rs`), as every read of the RPC layer; no change. |

## Checked and found correct (by the reviewer)

Idempotence of every linked step (fresh keys, `Busy`, `pending_key`, the lines' `busy()`); ticks serialised under `hold_tick`;
`pre_sign` arithmetic (supply after pending burns, payout guard, `eventual`); units in the pool desk (lamports per whole wX, u128);
`saturating_sub` uses are clamps; the raffle round's ordering (publish before commit, commit at a later slot, the pinned round
verified by two relays); the NFT flows' advance booked before its signature with an exact inverse; coin redemption's payout math;
the fee cash split; no trap on chain data (`sol_accounts` refuses a short answer).
