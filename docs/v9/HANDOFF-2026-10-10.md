## 1. Where Rook is going now (decision D-l, 2026-10-10)

**Canister-only Rook on Solana.** No Rook Solana programs (that avoided a ~12 SOL program-rent deposit). The Internet Computer
canister (`autobuyer/canister-rs`) does everything:

- **Wrapped tokens (wMILADY, wBOREDAPE, …)** are ordinary SPL tokens; the **canister's threshold key is the mint authority**.
- **Collection memecoins** launch on **Meteora DBC with the wrapped token as the quote** → they trade as memecoin/wX from day 1
  and graduate into **memecoin/wX DAMM v2 pools** (LP locked). The DBC **fee claimer is the canister's wallet**; 50 % of each raise
  (the migration fee, paid in wX) becomes that coin's backing.
- **The floor module runs inside the canister** (mint/burn wX at the floor ± spread, backing/reserve/surplus books, caps).
- **wX/SOL pools are Meteora DLMM pairs** (preset 262: bin step 50, 0.4 % fee, SOL-only fees), ≈10 SOL a side in a ±10 % band,
  re-centred in place by the canister (spike S4). They **grow from fees** (no seed money); the site's own two-hop buy
  (SOL → wX → coin) always works, Jupiter routes during each token's 14–30-day grace period and after once the pool is deep enough.
- **NFTs** are held by the canister (Ethereum, Ordinals, Solana wallets). 1 wX → a real NFT on redemption.
- **ROOK** (genesis coin) is SOL-paired; its fee claimer is the canister, so its fees fund Rook's operations.
- **Bootstrap paid by the operator: ≈ 0.2 SOL** (ROOK + a float for about eight openings). Each collection costs ≈ 0.023 SOL to
  open, paid from the float and then from fees.
- **No launch set (2026-10-12, "the people choose"):** the ceremony pins every working collection (11 Ethereum, Bitcoin Frogs,
  12 Solana: 24); each opens when a creator first asks (`coins_request_open`); the other Ordinals follow as their floor feeds pass.

Full design: **`docs/v9/canister-only-solana.md`** (§16 operator decisions, §17 the DLMM pool decision, the "Revision after spikes"
section). Spikes: `docs/v9/spikes/s1-s3-canister-only.md`, `docs/v9/spikes/s4-wx-pools.md`.
