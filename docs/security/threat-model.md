# Threat model

Written in Phase 3 (docs/phase3.md). Not an audit. For every actor: what they can **take**, what they can **block**, what they can
**degrade**, and what stops them. "Nothing" means no path was found, not that none exists.

| Actor | Take | Block | Degrade | What stops them |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Owner** (a multisig) | Nothing from a vault: no function sends ETH to the owner or anywhere the owner names. The treasury's 20% is the owner's to redirect, after 7 public days. | Everything new: pause a listing (no declaration, no release), cancel a declaration, never accept a root. | Appoint a colluding keeper, purchaser and attestor together (7 public days); list a collection with a false membership root (7 days to change one; the first is immediate); void an unclaimed draw after a week, which is a second draw; lower the bond requirement (7 days). | Delays on every loosening, immediacy only on tightening; nothing it can do moves ETH. Its real power is appointment, and that is the trust model's first line. |
| **Keeper** | Nothing directly. | Stop posting caps, snapshots, minimums: nothing new happens, nothing is lost. | Post a cap that is too high (bounded by its own 1 h TTL and by the purchaser's bond), publish a wrong holder snapshot (15 public minutes, recomputable byte for byte with `tools/snapshot`, cancellable by the owner), declare more payers than fit (refused at release; the owner cancels). | It never holds money. A bad snapshot is visible and reproducible, not rejected on chain — the design's stated residual trust. |
| **Purchaser** | Released ETH, up to `MAX_OPEN` (3) declarations each within cap × quantity — **against a bond of their own** that a write-off pays to the coins that were charged. At the default 100% bond, theft costs the thief what was taken. | Nothing: an overdue receipt stops new declarations until it is settled or written off; after 7 more days anyone writes it off. | Buy badly within the cap; deliver fewer than declared (cannot be closed until the difference is returned — H2). | The bond, per purchaser (M1); the cap; the hour; the settlement rule. Not stopped: a purchaser with a bond below 100% (if the owner lowered it) keeping the uncovered part. |
| **Attestor** | Nothing. | Refuse to receipt: the purchase goes overdue and is written off against the purchaser's bond. | **Lie**: receipt an inscription that did not arrive (must still be a member of the committed collection and not already held) or a rune amount that did not. | Must be a different key from purchaser and keeper (registry). Its word is checkable by anyone against the listing's public Bitcoin address; no contract checks it. Residual trust, stated. |
| **Coin creator** | Nothing. | Their own coin's acquisitions, by never choosing (I3). | Choose `Named` and name themselves: allowed, it is their coin's asset, and it is public. | Cannot touch another coin's acquisitions (`creatorOf`). Cannot re-point their router (no such function; Pons's own recipient transfer is never called). |
| **Any caller** | Nothing. | Nothing lasting. | Call `harvest`, `release`, `requestDraw`, `resolve`, `claimWin`, `claimShare` for anybody: each does the same thing whoever calls. Return 1 wei to front-run an exact refund (I1). | Dust cannot enter the queue (H1). Timing a draw needs the chain's clock a minute wrong (M2). |
| **Pons's protocol owner** | **Every future creator fee of any coin**, by redirecting its recipient — after Pons's own 3-day public timelock. | The same. | Change launch terms (the creator can pin them with `expectedEconomics`). | Nothing of ours. Fees simply stop arriving; what is already in a vault is unaffected. Stated in the router's NatSpec and the trust model. |
| **Pons's sweep operator** | Nothing (it credits escrow; it does not hold). | Post-graduation fees, by not sweeping a pool whose pending fees include the coin side — our router is refused there (docs/phase1.md). | Delay. | Nothing of ours. Measured active: median 84 s between sweeps. A dependency, not a guarantee. |
| **A malicious token** sent to a router | Nothing: `forwardToken` moves the router's own balance of that token to the treasury with `SafeERC20`; a token that lies about its balance or reverts only fails its own call. | Nothing: `harvest` never touches tokens. | A re-entrant token could call `harvest` mid-transfer: harmless, it is ETH-only and guarded. | No swap is ever made by a contract of ours, so there is nothing to sandwich. |
| **A malicious listing** | Nothing: a listing's vault is deployed by the registry, never supplied. | — | A misleading name or a root over the wrong inscriptions — the owner's act, public, slow to change. | The collection indexer at UniSat is an independent check of membership; it is one indexer's opinion. |
| **A griefing claimant** | Nothing. | Nothing: a claim only ever credits the named holder; a wrong proof reverts; a second claim reverts. | Claim *for* somebody who wanted to wait: harmless, it accrues to them. | `shareClaimed`, the `OverTotal` ceiling, `claimsEnd`. |
| **drand down or retired** | Nothing. | Draws, while it is down. | — | A pinned round that never arrives can be re-pinned by anyone after 24 h. If evmnet were retired the Distributor's beacon is immutable: a new Distributor would be needed, and draws already open would wait. The chain supports the BLS12-381 precompiles, so another drand network is possible. **Accepted, and a question for the auditor.** |
| **A reorg on 4663** | Nothing: no function's safety depends on a block hash or on an event having been final. | — | A `requestDraw` reorged into a later block pins a later round — still a future one. The keeper's *off-chain* acts (buying on Bitcoin after a `Released` event) must wait for finality: 4663 is an Arbitrum Nitro chain, soft-final at the sequencer and hard-final on L1. | The keeper's policy, not a contract: act on L1-final events only. Listed for Phase 4. |

## What no contract here can ever do

Make the purchaser buy. Know that an inscription arrived. Know who held a coin at a block. Make the keeper deliver. Stop Pons's
owner. Each of those is a person's word made public, slow, bonded or reproducible — never removed.
